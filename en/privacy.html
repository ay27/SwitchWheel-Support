How information is handled

This policy covers the SwitchWheel macOS app, these support pages, and information you voluntarily submit when requesting support.

The app does not send your keyboard input, running-app list, fixed positions, or app-switching history to the developer. This version has no network client entitlement and includes no ads, tracking, cloud sync, developer telemetry service, or embedded analytics SDK.

Information stored locally

To restore your configuration and help troubleshoot issues, the app stores the following in local macOS app preferences:

InformationPurpose
Activation mode, hold key, recorded shortcut, and its display labelRecognize your selected activation gesture
Fixed positions, selected app paths, security-scoped bookmarks, and retained fixed-position configurationRestore positions and the read-only access you granted
A stable ordering of app identitiesAvoid reordering apps by most recent use after each switch
Theme, inclusion of other running apps, and onboarding stateKeep your interface and usage preferences
Permission and listener status, modifier-event count, and app versionLocal troubleshooting

The app reads running-app names, identities, icons, and the pointer position needed for the gesture in memory to draw the wheel and switch apps. The app does not upload this runtime information. Stable app ordering is for layout consistency, not a timestamped log of switches.

Input & app access

Input Monitoring: Hold mode uses a listen-only event tap to recognize your selected left-hand modifier and other key or click events that cancel a gesture. The tap can receive key-down events, but the app does not read, record, or store the text of ordinary keyboard input or build a typing history. A modifier-event count is stored locally for troubleshooting.

Recording a shortcut: The key code, modifiers, and label of the shortcut you intentionally record are saved as settings so you can open the wheel again.

File access: Apps you choose or drag into fixed positions use sandboxed read-only access and security-scoped bookmarks. Access is used for app information and icons; it does not grant arbitrary access to document contents.

The app does not request Screen Recording or Accessibility permission and does not capture window previews or screen contents.

Sharing & third parties

This version has no feature that sends the local information above to the developer, advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. It does not sell or share that local information.

App Store purchases, refunds, distribution, and Apple system diagnostics or TestFlight feedback that you choose to provide are handled by Apple under its policies, rather than by an embedded developer analytics service. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Retention & your controls

Local preferences remain until changed, reset, or removed from your Mac. You can remove fixed apps, change shortcuts or themes, and revoke Input Monitoring in System Settings → Privacy & Security to disable hold mode. Setting fixed positions to 0 disables the feature; it does not erase all retained configuration.

macOS may retain preferences after uninstalling the app. Use the support channel for version-specific instructions to fully reset them. The developer does not receive this local data and cannot remotely view or erase it. The app has no account to delete.

Website & support requests

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages themselves contain no analytics, ads, forms, cookies, or local storage. The site uses the account’s existing ay27.com domain with Cloudflare proxying. GitHub and Cloudflare may process request information such as IP addresses and access logs under their policies. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you voluntarily open a GitHub issue, GitHub processes your account name, message, and attachments, and they appear publicly on the issue. The developer uses the information you supply to reply, troubleshoot, or maintain the app. Do not post private or sensitive information in public issues.

Use GitHub’s editing, deletion, and privacy controls to manage what you submit, or contact us through the support channel with a privacy request. The developer cannot control information retained by GitHub under its policies.

Changes & contact

If our data practices change, we will update the effective date on this page and, where appropriate, communicate changes in release notes. Before adding networking, sync, or analytics features, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy declaration accordingly.
